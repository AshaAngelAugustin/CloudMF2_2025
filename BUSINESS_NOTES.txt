DATABASE CONVERTER - BUSINESS ANALYSIS
========================================

Business Opportunity / Market Potential:
• High demand in enterprise environments for database migration and modernization projects
• Growing market as companies move from legacy systems (Oracle/Sybase) to cloud-based solutions

Ease of Implementation:
• Simple web-based tool requiring minimal infrastructure - just HTML/CSS/JavaScript
• Can be deployed quickly on any web server or integrated into existing enterprise tools

Scalable / Reusable:
• Core conversion logic can be extended to support additional database formats (MySQL, PostgreSQL)
• Template-based approach allows easy addition of new syntax conversion rules

Financial Feasibility:
• Low development and maintenance costs due to simple technology stack
• High ROI potential as it reduces manual effort in expensive database migration projects
